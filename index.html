A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Before you get in touch.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Include your postcode, the rooms or areas involved, the current condition of the surfaces and your preferred timing. Photos of the space can help — add them when you send your message on Instagram.

Can I enquire about a commercial project?

Yes. Calvin’s profile lists commercial painting, private developments, education and healthcare. Include the type of premises, approximate scope and any access or scheduling requirements in your enquiry.

Where can I see Calvin’s actual work?

Visit @calvinpaintingdecorating on Instagram for the company’s posts and reels. The images on this concept website are generated visual inspiration, rather than a portfolio of completed work.
